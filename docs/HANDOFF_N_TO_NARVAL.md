## N on Narval starts from scratch

Run all of N on Narval, rounds 1-10 of both models for all four plantings, in
fresh directories. Do not copy or pool anything from Trillium's N:

- the plan keeps each experiment on one cluster;
- `planted_feature.npz` and `synthetic_labels.npz` stay out of git, and the
  ABMIL rounds must use the same planting as the graph rounds, so the planting
  is regenerated by Narval's graph round 1;
- redoing the 11 graph rounds costs about 8 GPU-hours on Trillium's timings.

Trillium's partial N rounds stay in Trillium `/scratch/sorkwos/planted_v3_*` and
are unused.

## Walltimes

`walltime()` in `slurm/launch_review_runs.sh` now depends on the cluster.
Narval keeps the original values sized from `docs/NARVAL_ROUND_TIME_BUDGETS.txt`
(N graph 8 h BLCA / 12 h BRCA, N ABMIL 12 h / 16 h). Trillium uses shorter ones
from its step-1 timings: Trillium ran a BLCA ABMIL round in 38 min and a BRCA
one in 85 min, against ~4 h and 6-8 h on Narval.
